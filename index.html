ABOUT BRIGADE NEOPOLIS

Privilege, as the Gateway to True Distinction.

Brigade Gateway is a legacy reimagined. Two decades after redefining Bengaluru's skyline with the original Brigade Gateway—an integrated address of homes, hospitality, business, and retail—Brigade Group brings the same audacious vision to Hyderabad.

Set within Neopolis, Kokapet, this is a world where residences, a World Trade Center, five-star hospitality, and curated retail exist within a single, self-contained universe. It isn't just where you live. It's where the city comes to you.

True Luxury Is Always Private.

The Private Residences at Brigade Gateway are a bespoke island of luxury, designed to indulge your finer tastes—spaciousness at the edge of lavish, grandeur on the spectrum of royalty, and thoughtfulness in the realm of art. Every residence is designed for those who consider space, light, and privacy non-negotiable, set high above Hyderabad's fastest-growing business corridor.

Brigade Group – A Legacy of Excellence

BRIGADE GROUP

For nearly four decades, Brigade Group has been shaping South India's skyline with iconic residential, commercial, hospitality, retail, and mixed-use developments. Since its inception in 1986, the Group has built a reputation founded on quality, innovation, trust, and timeless design, creating landmark destinations that redefine urban living.

From pioneering integrated townships and World Trade Centers to award-winning residential communities and luxury hospitality destinations, Brigade continues to deliver thoughtfully crafted spaces that enrich lives and build enduring communities. Today, its developments span multiple cities across South India, reflecting a commitment to excellence in every square foot.
